  gaussian_blur: 3x3 kernel, sigma 0.35-0.85; or

  sensor_noise: zero-mean Gaussian noise, stddev 3.0-7.0.

Category 3 / suffix _aug3_mild_geometry_<operation>: one label-synchronised geometric operation.

  horizontal flip, rotation within +/-5 degrees, translation within +/-2%, or scale 1.04-1.10.

  Vertical flipping is not used.
